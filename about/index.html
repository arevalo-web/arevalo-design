Hello!

I've always had an interest in art—honestly, I just liked things that looked cool. My interest in design really grew at UNLV. I initially didn't have design as my major, but after switching over, I soon fell in love with it. Learning from great teachers and legends of the field really helped me understand how design is done. I hope to use my design knowledge to branch into different areas of the industry; whether it's UI/UX or production-based design, I'm always ready to learn new things. When I'm not focusing on design, I enjoy spending my time painting, lifting, or watching scary movies—Hereditary being my favorite. Ultimately, I hope to find a career where I can continue to grow and learn.
