DECEMBER 2025 ROLLOUT

A conceptual visual re-release of Lil Uzi Vert's Luv Is Rage 2, reimagining the album’s identity while maintaining the artist's distinctive style.

THE PROBLEM

Lil Uzi Vert’s aesthetic is defined by vibrant colors, surreal environments, and cosmic themes. The challenge was to create a fresh, unexpected visual direction that still felt authentic to his established brand.

THE RESULT

A monochromatic, ethereal interpretation of the album’s space-inspired themes. Influenced by cyber sigilism, the design transforms the original vibrant energy into a darker, atmospheric system extending across packaging and merchandise.
